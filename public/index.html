Liquidations

Funding is exchanged every 8 hours between longs and shorts. Positive funding means longs pay shorts (crowded longs); negative means shorts pay longs. Open interest and long/short ratios come from Binance futures. Liquidations stream live from Bybit and are counted from when the server started.
